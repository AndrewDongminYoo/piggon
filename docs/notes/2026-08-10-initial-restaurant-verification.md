# Initial restaurant verification

Verified on 2026-08-11 for the 23 restaurant records approved for the MVP seed.
Kakao fields were taken from the public Kakao Map search result for the exact venue and cross-checked against the returned place name, road address, place ID, latitude, and longitude.
`openoff_status=Y` is recorded below as an active Kakao listing with operating hours, not as a guarantee that the business will remain open after the verification date.
YouTube authors and titles were resolved through YouTube oEmbed before a video was treated as a Pizza Ggondae channel source.
AVPN claims require a direct restaurant entry in the official AVPN directory, award claims require an organizer result, and popup availability requires dated operator or venue evidence.

## Decision rules

- `published` means the current venue name and exact Kakao location were verified and every attribute inserted into the public seed has a direct source.
- A restaurant may be published without an unverified award or supplied video claim; the unsupported attribute or video relation is omitted from the seed.
- `[PARTIAL]` means the supplied claim could not be completed from the required direct source.
- `[UNCERTAIN]` means the supplied source conflicts with the intended public claim.
- `[OUT_OF_SCOPE]` is reserved for a source-verified retired venue; no supplied venue met that condition.
